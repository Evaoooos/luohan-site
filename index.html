Luohan

One sage, one verse, each day.

An old counting custom, five hundred verses, and six seconds of ink.

In the halls of the five hundred sages, a visitor picks any statue as a starting point and counts along the rows up to their own age. The one they land on is theirs for the day. Luohan brings that custom to your phone.

Take any number, add your age. Tap once: the words melt into ink and sink, a drop blooms through the water, a paper slip rises out of it, and the letters bleed in one by one.

What it holds

  • Five hundred verses, with a name and a number each
  • Passages from the Tao Te Ching, the Analects, Zhuangzi and the Heart Sutra, in public-domain translations
  • One quiet minute — sit with a passage while an ink dot breathes
  • A line for today — one sentence, kept on your phone
  • A home screen widget with today's sage

The full reading

Each sage has a reflection and five readings — Work, People, Choices, Rest, Timing — about a thousand words, each ending with one small step for today. One purchase unlocks all five hundred, for good. No subscription.

Luohan makes no predictions and no promises about the future. It offers old words, and room to think.
