Arhat

Privacy

No accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.

Arhat does not send your journal or activity to us. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your phone

Everything the app remembers is stored on your device only: the sage you drew today, your streak, the lines you keep, the minutes you have sat with a passage, and your reminder setting. The app shares only your latest draw’s number and date with its home-screen widget on the same device. Your journal is never shared with the widget or sent to us. Removing the app and its widget removes their local data; operating-system backups may retain earlier copies.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, your payment details, or your Apple Account. The app only asks Apple whether this device has the purchase, so it knows what to unlock.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally by iOS on your device. No notification is sent from a server, because there is no server.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use these to respond to your request. Please avoid sending private journal entries.

Children

The app does not request personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date below.
